Fix the booking availability time logic.

Current issues:

1. If the current time is 10:53, the app may still show 10:00 or 10:30 slots. Past slots must never be shown or bookable.
2. Venue opening/closing times need to support closing after midnight, e.g. open 18:00 and close 02:00.
3. Availability must remain correct across midnight and not break when close time is technically earlier than open time.

Required behaviour:

A. Past slot filtering

* For the current date only, hide any slot whose start datetime is earlier than the actual current datetime.
* Example:
    * Current time = 10:53
    * Hide 10:00 and 10:30
    * Show 11:00 only if available.
* Also prevent backend booking creation for any slot in the past, even if the UI is manipulated.

B. Future dates

* Do not apply current-time filtering to future dates.
* Show all slots that match venue opening hours and availability.

C. Overnight opening hours
Support venues that close after midnight.
Examples:

* open 18:00, close 02:00 should create slots from 18:00 today through 02:00 tomorrow.
* open 20:00, close 01:00 should create slots from 20:00 today through 01:00 tomorrow.
* open 09:00, close 17:00 should continue working normally.

D. Slot ownership/date logic

* Overnight slots after midnight should still be part of the previous evening’s trading session where appropriate.
* Ensure availability checks compare full datetimes, not just time strings.

E. Conflict checks

* Keep existing confirmed and pending booking conflict checks.
* Do not show unavailable slots.
* Do not allow double-booking.

3. Venue open 12:00–24:00.
4. Venue open 18:00–02:00.
5. Venue open 20:00–01:00.
6. Booking across midnight works if within opening hours.
7. Booking in the past is rejected server-side.
